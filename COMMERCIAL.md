## 中文

webClx 使用 AGPL-3.0-or-later 开源。遵守该许可证的个人和组织可以自行部署、修改和使用。

以下服务可以单独付费购买：

- 一次性远程部署、安全加固与验收：人民币 299-699 元，按环境复杂度报价；
- 托管基础版：人民币 49 元/月或 8 美元/月起；
- 托管专业版：人民币 129 元/月或 19 美元/月起；
- 小团队隔离实例与支持：人民币 399 元/月或 59 美元/月起；
- 无法履行 AGPL 网络源码义务的商业集成：单独协商商业许可证。

模型调用费、云主机、域名和第三方服务费不包含在上述价格中。正式报价会写清实例规格、支持范围、备份和退出方式，不承诺无限模型额度。

### 7 天独立托管试用

[加入试用候补](https://github.com/beyondcy1013/webClx/issues/new?template=hosted-trial.yml)。当前由维护者逐一确认容量、地区和开通时间，提交申请不代表实例已经可用。获准开通后，每位试用者使用独立 OS 用户、端口、工作区、登录凭据和服务实例。试用期结束后提供 7 天只读导出窗口，随后删除实例数据。

不要在公开 Issue 中提交密码、Cookie、API Key、私有仓库地址或客户数据。模型凭据应在实例交付后由试用者自行配置。

## English

webClx is open source under AGPL-3.0-or-later. Individuals and organizations may self-host, modify, and use it when they comply with that license.

Paid services are available separately:

- one-time remote deployment, hardening, and acceptance testing: CNY 299-699 depending on the environment;
- hosted starter: from CNY 49 or USD 8 per month;
- hosted professional: from CNY 129 or USD 19 per month;
- isolated small-team instance and support: from CNY 399 or USD 59 per month;
- commercial licensing for integrations that cannot meet AGPL network-source obligations: quoted separately.

Model usage, cloud infrastructure, domains, and third-party services are billed separately. A formal quote states instance resources, support scope, backups, and exit terms; it never implies unlimited model usage.

### Seven-day isolated hosted trial

[Join the trial waitlist](https://github.com/beyondcy1013/webClx/issues/new?template=hosted-trial.yml). Capacity, region, and activation time are confirmed manually; submitting the form does not mean an instance is already available. Once approved, each trial receives an isolated OS user, port, workspace, login credential, and service instance. A seven-day read-only export window follows the trial before instance data is deleted.

Never place passwords, cookies, API keys, private repository URLs, or customer data in a public issue. Trial users configure model credentials themselves after instance delivery.
